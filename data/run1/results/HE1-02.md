# Quality of information check: eight voice AI interviews

## Source database

Each participant is one source, rated against H1 to H3. Corroboration means another participant says something consistent.

- **P1** (Q1, Q6). Completeness: medium. Q1 compares the AI with chatbots, not voice automation, so H1 is only partly addressed. Accuracy: self-report. Corroboration: none for Q1 or Q6.
- **P2** (Q2). Completeness: low, one H1 comment. Accuracy: self-report. Corroboration: consistent with Q3 only.
- **P3** (Q3). Completeness: low to medium, one H1 comment. Accuracy: self-report. Corroboration: consistent with Q2.
- **P4** (Q5, Q9). Completeness: medium, covers H3 and a changing reaction. Accuracy: hypothetical ("if it was myself", "If I was gonna do it"). Corroboration: Q9 is consistent with Q10 (P5) on asking for a human.
- **P5** (Q7, Q10). Completeness: medium, covers H1, H2 and H3. Accuracy: hypothetical ("if I was", "would've"). Corroboration: Q10 is consistent with Q9 (P4) and Q8 (P10).
- **P10** (Q4, Q8). Completeness: medium, covers H1 and H2. Accuracy: Q8 is a stated "would've", not observed behaviour. Corroboration: Q8 is partly matched by Q10 (P5, "probably").
- **P8, P9.** No quotes in the material. Completeness, accuracy and corroboration cannot be rated.

## What is known

- Both positive and negative H1 reactions appear (Q2, Q3 against Q4, Q6, Q7). The material does not show whether either group is larger.
- Two quoted participants say they would, or probably would, hang up (Q8, Q10), which counts against H2 for those two.
- Two participants say they would ask for a human (Q9, Q10), which counts against H3 for those two.

## What is unknown

- Any reaction from P8 and P9, and whether the ten quotes represent all eight participants.
- Whether anyone compared the AI with traditional voice automation, which is what H1 names.
- Actual behaviour. Participants only listened to prerecorded scenarios, so H2 and H3 rest on stated intentions.

## Items resting on single-source support

- The chatbot comparison (Q1, P1).
- Annoyance at the human-agent option (Q6, P1).
- The American accent concern (Q4, P10).
- The "cold" voice in a stressful claim (Q7, P5).
- Hanging up at the introduction (Q8, P10) is single-source for that specific point.

## Assumptions and limitations

The technique judges only the quotes supplied. It cannot judge full transcripts, quote selection or the unquoted participants, and it gives no verdict on H1 to H3.

## Alternative explanations

The weak support for H2 and H3 may reflect hypothetical phrasing ("would've") rather than real intent. The quotes may also be selected because they are vivid, which would overstate both praise and complaint.
