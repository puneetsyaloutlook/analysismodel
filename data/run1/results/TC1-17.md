## Does the conclusion reverse?

Dropping a participant does not reverse any conclusion. H3 weakens when P4 or P5 is removed but still has hedged evidence against it, and H1 stays mixed and untested in every row.

The definitions matter. Under the strict readings, H2 and H3 are no longer contradicted, because no quote meets the strict test. That is a reversal from "contradicted" to "not contradicted", and it does not mean the hypotheses are supported. Dropping P10 alone also removes the only quote that fits the immediate timing for H2.

The favoured reading for H2 and H3 therefore depends on counting hypothetical, hedged or untimed statements as evidence. Rows were varied one at a time, so each change can be traced to its cause.

## Assumptions and limitations

The re-runs use only the ten quotes and the findings' own classifications. P8 and P9 cannot be dropped because they have no quotes. Quote selection is unknown. Leave-one-out tests dependence on individuals, not representativeness. Participants heard prerecorded scenarios, so every behavioural quote is an intention.

## Alternative explanations

The strict-definition result may show that the hypotheses are worded too tightly for hypothetical interview data, not that customers behave differently. Q8 may reflect the length of the introduction, and Q7 the distress of a car accident, so the effects in the original reading may belong to the scenarios and not to voice AI.
