# Material: eight customer interviews on voice AI customer support

Eight participants (P1, P2, P3, P4, P5, P8, P9, P10) took part in semi-structured interviews. Each listened to four prerecorded voice AI scenarios (motor claim lodgement, policy sales, premium disputes, policy cancellation) and then gave reactions and preferences. Only some participants are quoted below. Quotes are verbatim from the transcripts, in no particular order.

## Hypotheses to test

- H1: Customers find voice AI interactions more satisfying than traditional voice automation.
- H2: Customers do not hang up immediately when they learn they are speaking with an AI.
- H3: Customers do not immediately ask for a human agent.
