    AO-02 Power-interest grid (influence-interest grid, power-impact grid)

    Plots actors on power and interest to choose an engagement strategy for each, producing a two by two grid.

    Assess actors, options and risksEvidence: DocumentedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Plots stakeholders on power and interest to choose an engagement strategy for each. Artefact: a two by two grid; read who needs close engagement and who needs monitoring.

    When to use it

    Use when a user has a list of actors and asks which need close engagement and which only monitoring.

    When it does not fit

    Stakeholder positions change quickly, so a single plot goes out of date.

    Result format

    The result is a two by two grid with power on one axis and interest on the other, with each stakeholder placed in a quadrant. It names the stakeholders who need close engagement and those who need monitoring, with the engagement strategy for each quadrant.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failurePlaces actors in quadrants with false precision from titles alone and presents the grid as fact.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops because placement is judgemental (risk of bias) and because the grid is dated as soon as positions change (indirectness).

    Related techniques

    • should follow AO-01 Stakeholder analysis (stakeholder mapping): the register supplies the actors to place
    • pairs with AO-03 Stakeholder salience model (Mitchell, Agle and Wood, 1997): salience adds legitimacy and urgency, which a two-axis grid omits

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. Attributed to Mendelow (1991), whose reference is missing from the page; placement is judgemental, and the usual quadrant labels were not seen in a fetched source. (Wikipedia, Stakeholder analysis)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
