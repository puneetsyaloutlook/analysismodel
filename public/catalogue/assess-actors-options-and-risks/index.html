    Assess actors, options and risks

    19 techniques.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    AO-01Stakeholder analysis (stakeholder mapping)Identifies who is affected by or can affect a topic and classifies each by interest, influence and attitude, producing a stakeholder register and grid.Documented
    AO-02Power-interest grid (influence-interest grid, power-impact grid)Plots actors on power and interest to choose an engagement strategy for each, producing a two by two grid.Documented
    AO-03Stakeholder salience model (Mitchell, Agle and Wood, 1997)Ranks actors by legitimacy, power and urgency, producing a diagram with eight classes from definitive to non-stakeholder.Documented
    AO-04Political economy analysisExplains why a problem persists by analysing structures, institutions, actors, interests, incentives and power relations, producing a synthesis of key propositions, pathways of change, and maps of blockers and champions.Documented
    AO-05Customer journey mappingTurns a persona goals, actions, thoughts and emotions across stages into a visual map, producing a map with phases, touchpoints, pain points and opportunities.Documented
    AO-06PersonasCondenses research on user groups into a fictional but realistic description, producing a persona profile with goals, context and concerns.Documented
    AO-07Jobs to be done (JTBD)Frames demand around the progress a customer is trying to make in a circumstance, producing a job statement that shows which jobs are poorly served.Practice
    AO-08Eightfold path (Bardach)Structures a policy question into eight steps from defining the problem to telling the story, producing a written options analysis with criteria, projected outcomes and trade-offs.Documented
    AO-09Longlist-to-shortlist options analysis (options appraisal)Generates a wide set of options, filters them against objectives and critical success factors, and appraises a shortlist of about five, producing a longlist and shortlist with red, amber and green ratings.Documented
    AO-10Cost-benefit analysisValues all costs and benefits to society in money, discounts them and compares options by net present value, producing net present value per option with sensitivity results.Documented
    AO-11Cost-effectiveness and cost-utility analysisCompares options on cost per unit of one quantified effect when benefits cannot be monetised, producing cost per unit of effect for each option.Documented
    AO-12Multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA)Scores options against several weighted criteria, producing weights, scores and a ranking, with a test of how the ranking changes when weights change.Documented
    AO-13Decision tree and expected valueLays out decisions, chance events and outcomes and computes expected value or utility, producing a decision tree that shows the alternative with the best expected value.Documented
    AO-14Real options analysisValues the right but not the obligation to expand, delay, switch or abandon an investment, producing an option value added to a standard valuation.Unconfirmed
    AO-15Risk matrix (likelihood and consequence matrix, risk heat map)Rates each risk by plotting likelihood against consequence, producing a grid of ratings that shows which risks need treatment first.Documented
    AO-16Failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA)Lists the failure modes of each function or component with causes and effects and ranks them, producing a worksheet with ranked failure modes that shows single points of failure.Documented
    AO-17Bow-tie analysisMaps causes, a central event, consequences and the barriers on each path, producing a cause, event and consequence diagram that shows pathways lacking a barrier.Documented
    AO-18Threat modelling (STRIDE: spoofing, tampering, repudiation, information disclosure, denial of service, elevation of privilege)Identifies security threats using six categories mapped to security properties, producing a list of threats by category.Documented
    AO-19Ranking uncertainty analysis (stochastic multicriteria acceptability analysis; SMAA; rank acceptability index)Samples weights and score uncertainty many times and reports how often each option takes each rank, giving rank acceptability indices that show how stable a multi-criteria ranking is.Documented
