    AO-08 Eightfold path (Bardach)

    Structures a policy question into eight steps from defining the problem to telling the story, producing a written options analysis with criteria, projected outcomes and trade-offs.

    Assess actors, options and risksEvidence: DocumentedPrimary technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Structures a policy question into eight steps from defining the problem to telling the story. Artefact: a written options analysis with criteria, projected outcomes and trade-offs.

    When to use it

    Use when a user asks which policy option to recommend and needs a step-by-step structure from problem definition to recommendation.

    When it does not fit

    Projected outcomes cannot be estimated because evidence is missing.

    Result format

    The result is a written options analysis that follows the eight steps from defining the problem to telling the story. It states the criteria, the projected outcome of each option against them and the trade-offs, with a comparison matrix of options against criteria.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureFills the projected outcomes step with unsupported estimates and skips the step that confronts the trade-offs.
    Error sourcesinput data; source selection; coder or rater judgement; expert opinion
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when projected outcomes rest on weak evidence (indirectness), when criteria are chosen after the preferred option is known (risk of bias), and when step outputs and criticisms were not verified.

    Related techniques

    • should follow FQ-02 Decision-first framing (decision-focused or value-focused question design): a decision-first frame states the decision that the analysis serves
    • pairs with AO-12 Multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA): explicit weights make the trade-off step transparent
    • pairs with AO-10 Cost-benefit analysis: monetised outcomes can fill the projection step when benefits can be valued

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. Step names verified on a secondary page; step-level outputs and criticisms were not. (Wikipedia, Eightfold path (policy analysis))

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
