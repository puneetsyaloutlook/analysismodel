    AO-09 Longlist-to-shortlist options analysis (options appraisal)

    Generates a wide set of options, filters them against objectives and critical success factors, and appraises a shortlist of about five, producing a longlist and shortlist with red, amber and green ratings.

    Assess actors, options and risksEvidence: DocumentedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Generates a wide set of options, filters them against objectives and critical success factors, and appraises a shortlist of about five. Artefact: a longlist and shortlist with red, amber, green ratings.

    When to use it

    Use when there are more candidates than can be appraised in detail, such as projects or initiatives competing for funding, and a defensible shortlist is needed before detailed scoring.

    When it does not fit

    Objectives are output-based or not measurable, so options cannot be screened.

    Result format

    The result has a longlist of options and a shortlist of about five, with a red, amber or green rating for each option against each objective and critical success factor. It states why each option was dropped or kept, in a comparison matrix.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureScreens options against vague objectives so that ratings are asserted and not tested, and drops an option without a recorded reason.
    Error sourcesinput data; source selection; coder or rater judgement; expert opinion
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when screening criteria are not measurable (indirectness), when options were generated by a narrow group (risk of bias), and when only part of the guidance was read.

    Related techniques

    • should follow FQ-02 Decision-first framing (decision-focused or value-focused question design): the decision and its objectives set the screening criteria
    • pairs with AO-12 Multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDA): weighted scoring appraises the shortlist more finely than red, amber and green ratings
