    Hold the report to its evidence

    These techniques check a report against its evidence: detecting spin, stating limitations and caveats, comparing a report with its pre-registration and checking it against a reporting guideline. Step 8 chooses from them to check the written answer before it is given.

    5 techniques. Open one to see what it does, when it fits and what it needs from your material.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    RC-01Spin detection and classification (spin in systematic reviews; Yavchitz classification)Checks the title, abstract, conclusion and recommendations for wording that makes results look more favourable than they are, using 39 types in three categories: misleading reporting, misleading interpretation and inappropriate extrapolation.Documented
    RC-02Limitations and caveats statement (assumption and limitation statement)States what the analysis cannot show, why, and which conclusion each limitation weakens, and checks that summaries keep those limitations.Documented
    RC-03Registered report and pre-registration comparison (pre-registration; registered reports; protocol registration)Fixes the question, hypotheses and methods before results are seen, with peer review and in-principle acceptance for registered reports, and compares the final report with that plan.Tested
    RC-04Reporting guideline checklist (EQUATOR network guidelines such as CONSORT, PRISMA 2020, STROBE and CHEERS)Checks a report against a checklist of what a report of its study type must contain.Documented
    RC-05Qualitative reporting standards (SRQR (Standards for Reporting Qualitative Research); COREQ (Consolidated criteria for Reporting Qualitative research))Checks a qualitative report against reporting items, with COREQ covering interviews and focus groups, including researcher reflexivity.Documented
