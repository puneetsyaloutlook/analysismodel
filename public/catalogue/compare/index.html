    Compare

    17 techniques.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    CM1-01Historical analogy and parallel analysis (likenesses and differences, analogical explanation)Places the present topic beside a past case and rates each likeness and difference, producing a likenesses and differences table that shows where a lesson from the past case may and may not be applied.Documented
    CM1-02Mill's method of agreement and most different systems design (MDSD)Looks across dissimilar cases that share an outcome for the factor present in all of them, producing a table of candidate factors by case in which the common factor is the candidate cause.Documented
    CM1-03Mill's method of difference and most similar systems design (MSSD; joint method of agreement and difference)Looks for the factor present where an outcome occurs and absent in an otherwise similar case where it does not, producing a short list of the remaining differences between matched cases.Documented
    CM1-04Concomitant variation and method of residues (Mill)Infers a link when a factor varies with the effect (concomitant variation) or attributes the unexplained part of an effect to the remaining circumstances (residues), producing a co-movement pattern or a residual.Documented
    CM1-05Comparative case study (structured focused comparison, small-N comparison)Examines a small number of cases with the same questions for each, producing a case by case matrix that shows consistent patterns and deviant cases.Documented
    CM1-06Case selection techniques (extreme, deviant, diverse, index, most similar)Chooses which cases to compare by their position on a variable distribution or on a model prediction, producing a chosen case set with the rule used.Unconfirmed
    CM1-07Qualitative comparative analysis (QCA; crisp-set, fuzzy-set and multi-value variants)Uses set theory and Boolean algebra to find combinations of conditions that are necessary or sufficient for an outcome across a medium number of cases, producing a truth table and minimal solution formulas with consistency and coverage scores.Documented
    CM1-08Cross-case synthesis (replication logic)Combines completed within-case write-ups into one cross-case matrix, producing a matrix that shows repeating patterns and diverging cases.Unconfirmed
    CM1-09Contrast with a structural opposite (term from earlier work)Sets the topic beside a case or position that differs from it on a stated defining structure, producing a table of contrasting features that shows what each assumes or lacks.Unconfirmed
    CM1-10Cross-sectional and longitudinal comparison (cohort and panel comparison; cross-cycle comparison)Compares different units at one time (cross-sectional) or the same units repeatedly (longitudinal), producing group differences at one time or change paths over time.Documented
    CM1-11Before and after comparison (pre-post design)Compares an outcome for the same unit before and after an event, producing a change estimate.Unconfirmed
    CM1-12Benchmarking and peer comparison (best-practice comparison, competitor analysis)Compares an organisation processes and metrics with those of peers or leaders, producing a gap table of own against benchmark performance.Documented
    CM1-13Forecaster and source comparison (forecast scoring, aggregation, track record comparison) Also serves: Project forward.Scores a forecast against those of other forecasters on resolved outcomes and aggregates them, producing an accuracy ranking and an aggregate forecast.Tested
    CM1-14Absence analysis (argument from silence, the dog that did not bark) Also serves: Handle evidence.Draws a conclusion from an item missing from a document or comparison set where it would be expected if a claim were true, producing a list of expected but absent items.Documented
    CM1-15Gap analysisCompares current with desired performance, producing a prioritised list of gaps and the means to close each.Documented
    CM1-16Maturity model and capability assessment (Capability Maturity Model Integration, CMMI)Rates processes against staged levels, producing a maturity level or capability profile that shows where an organisation stands and what to improve.Documented
    CM-01Sponsor versus non-sponsor comparison (meta-epidemiological sponsorship analysis)Compares the share of favourable results and conclusions between sponsored and independently funded reports on one topic, producing a risk ratio or odds ratio.Documented
