    4. CM1-02 Mill's method of agreement and most different systems design (MDSD)

    CM1-02 Mill's method of agreement and most different systems design (MDSD)

    Looks across dissimilar cases that share an outcome for the factor present in all of them, producing a table of candidate factors by case in which the common factor is the candidate cause.

    CompareEvidence: DocumentedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: show a link between things

    What it does

    Looks for the factor present in every case that shows the outcome, across dissimilar contexts. Artefact: a table of candidate factors by case; the factor common to all is the candidate cause.

    When to use it

    Use when a user has several different cases with the same outcome and asks what they have in common.

    When it does not fit

    The candidate list is incomplete, the shared factor differs in kind from case to case, or the result is read as confirmation. Agreement only rules out factors that were absent when the effect occurred.

    Result format

    The result is a table of candidate factors by case, for cases that show the outcome in dissimilar contexts, with each cell showing whether the factor is present. It names the factor common to all cases as the candidate cause.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureDeclares the shared factor the cause without checking that the candidate list is complete, and treats factors that differ in kind as the same factor.
    Error sourcesinput data; source selection; coder or rater judgement
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when the candidate list may be incomplete or the outcome has several causes (indirectness), and when cases were selected on the outcome (risk of bias).

    Related techniques

    • should follow CM1-06 Case selection techniques (extreme, deviant, diverse, index, most similar): a stated case selection rule shows how the dissimilar cases were chosen
    • pairs with CM1-03 Mill's method of difference and most similar systems design (MSSD; joint method of agreement and difference): the method of difference adds a case without the outcome, which agreement alone lacks
    • pairs with TC1-04 Process tracing (diagnostic tests: straw in the wind, hoop, smoking gun, doubly decisive): the shared factor is only a candidate, and process tracing tests whether it operated

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. Described in an encyclopaedia entry and a design summary of Przeworski and Teune (1970). Plurality-of-causes criticisms were not in a retrieved source. (Britannica, Mill's methods; Wikipedia, Mill's Methods; Przeworski and Teune design summary)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
