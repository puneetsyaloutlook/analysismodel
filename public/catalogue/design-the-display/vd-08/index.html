    VD-08 Focus chart (highlight and grey context)

    Colours the series of interest and draws the others in light grey.

    Design the displayEvidence: DocumentedReport technique, runs in step 8

    What it does

    Colours the series of interest and draws the others in light grey. Artefact: one highlighted series against a background group. Read the highlighted series against the spread of the others.

    When to use it

    Use when a finding is shown as a chart or a graphic.

    When it does not fit

    Fits a story about one series compared with a group, and not one where several series matter equally.

    Result format

    The result is one highlighted series against a background group.
