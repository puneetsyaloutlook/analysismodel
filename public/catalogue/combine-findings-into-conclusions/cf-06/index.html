    CF-06 Best evidence synthesis (Slavin)

    Keeps only the best designed studies on a question under explicit inclusion criteria and describes them in detail, with effect sizes where available.

    Combine findings into conclusionsEvidence: DocumentedConclusion technique, runs in step 7

    What it does

    Keeps only the best designed studies on a question under explicit inclusion criteria and describes them in detail, with effect sizes where available. Artefact: a review of the retained studies. Read the quality threshold and when it was set.

    When to use it

    Use when the findings of two or more techniques must be turned into stated conclusions.

    When it does not fit

    Fits when many weak studies would dominate a pooled result, and fails when the threshold is set after seeing which studies agree.

    Result format

    The result is a review of the retained studies.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureApplies the quality threshold inconsistently across studies.
    Error sourceslanguage model; expert opinion
    Certainty limitsThe conclusion depends on the findings it combines. Certainty drops when the findings come from one technique, from the same material read the same way, or when a conflict between findings has no stated cause.

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. The 1986 paper proposes an alternative to meta-analytic and traditional reviews that uses a priori inclusion criteria, focuses on the studies highest in internal and external validity and uses effect sizes as an adjunct to a full discussion. Only an abstract on aggregator pages was read, and no test was found. (Slavin 1986, Educational Researcher; annotation in the CIRS bibliography)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
