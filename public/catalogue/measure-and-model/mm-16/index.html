    MM-16 Word embeddings for concept measurement and semantic change (diachronic embeddings; concept direction)

    Measure how terms relate in meaning within a corpus or across periods and produce nearest neighbours, similarity to seed words and change scores.

    Measure and modelEvidence: TestedPhase: analyseClaim ceiling: describe

    What it does

    Measures how terms relate in meaning within a corpus or across periods. Artefact: nearest neighbours, similarity to seed words and change scores. Read the neighbours before the scores.

    When to use it

    Use when you want to know whether the meaning or use of a term has shifted across periods or groups, or how close two concepts are in a corpus.

    When it does not fit

    Needs enough text per group or period and a stable method. Small corpora give unstable vectors.

    Output specification

    The result has the nearest neighbours of each term, the similarity of each term to the seed words, and a change score for each term across periods or groups. The neighbours are listed before the scores.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilitymodel plus code
    Typical model failureReports semantic change from a model that has not seen the domain.
    Error sourcesinput data; data transformation; statistical assumptions
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops with small corpora (imprecision), vectors that change with training settings (inconsistency) and a model that has not seen the domain (indirectness).

    Related techniques

    • should follow PE-02 Preprocessing sensitivity testing (text cleaning; tokenisation; stopword removal; stemming; boilerplate removal; preText): tokenisation and cleaning choices change the vectors
    • pairs with PE-06 Keyword in context (KWIC; concordance): keyword in context shows how the term is used for each neighbour
    • pairs with CK-08 Semantic, predictive and convergent validity checks (construct validity checks): predictive validity checks compare change scores with known events

    Evidence and tag basis

    Tested. Three embedding methods were tested against known historical shifts on six corpora, and less frequent and more polysemous words changed faster (2016, abstract). (Hamilton et al. 2016, arXiv 1605.09096)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
