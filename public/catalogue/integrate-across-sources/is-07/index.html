    IS-07 Convergent comparison of qualitative and quantitative results (convergent parallel design; concurrent triangulation)

    Analyses qualitative and quantitative results on the same topic separately, then merges them to compare and produces a comparison of agreement, partial agreement and discordance by topic.

    Integrate across sourcesEvidence: DocumentedPhase: analyseClaim ceiling: associate

    What it does

    Takes qualitative and quantitative results on the same topic from a similar period, analyses each, then merges the results to compare them. Artefact: a comparison of results. Read agreement, partial agreement and discordance for each topic.

    When to use it

    Use when you have interview or document findings and survey or data results on the same topic and need to see where they agree or conflict.

    When it does not fit

    Fits when both strands address the same constructs. Misfit: the strands measure different things, so discordance cannot be interpreted. Discordance needs follow-up: look for bias, check assumptions, gather more data, re-analyse, consult theory or question construct validity.

    Output specification

    The result has a comparison matrix with one row per topic, a column for the qualitative finding, a column for the quantitative finding and a column rating the pair as agreement, partial agreement or discordance. It ends with a list of the topics rated discordant, each with a note on what follow-up the discordance needs.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureReads partial agreement as full agreement and explains discordance without checking either strand for bias.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when the strands cover different time periods or constructs (indirectness) and when one strand is weak (risk of bias).

    Related techniques

    • pairs with IS-08 Joint display: a joint display shows the comparison
    • should follow HE1-13 Reflexive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke): the qualitative strand must be analysed before merging

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. The source states that the extent to which mixed methods studies implement integration remains limited. (Fetters, Curry and Creswell 2013, via core.ac.uk)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
