    PE-05 Missing data diagnosis and treatment (missingness analysis; imputation; complete case analysis; non-response weighting)

    Measure how much is missing, where and for whom, then choose deletion, imputation or weighting, giving the missing share by field and subgroup and a cleaned dataset.

    Prepare and extractEvidence: DocumentedPhase: verify and prepareClaim ceiling: describe

    What it does

    Measures how much is missing, where and for whom, then chooses deletion, imputation or weighting. Artefact: the missing share by field and subgroup, and a comparison of complete and incomplete cases. Read for systematic patterns.

    When to use it

    Use when records have blank or absent values and you must decide how to treat them before analysis.

    When it does not fit

    Fits when the missing share is similar across subgroups and unrelated to key variables. Misfit: missingness differs by outcome or group, so deletion biases results. The mechanism needs a person. The classification into missing completely at random, at random and not at random was not confirmed from a source.

    Output specification

    The result is a table of the missing share by field and by subgroup, and a comparison of complete and incomplete cases. It names the fields where missingness follows a systematic pattern and states the treatment chosen: deletion, imputation or weighting.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureDrops incomplete records or imputes means without testing whether missingness is related to the outcome.
    Error sourcesinput data; data transformation; statistical assumptions
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when the missingness mechanism cannot be judged (indirectness), when complete and incomplete cases differ (risk of bias), or when results change between treatments (inconsistency).

    Related techniques

    • should follow PE-04 Data profiling and rule-based validation (data audit; data quality assessment; edit checks): profiling locates the missing values and their patterns
    • pairs with MM-06 Survey weighting and subgroup estimation (design weights; calibration; post-stratification; relative standard error): survey weighting is the alternative treatment for non-response in survey records

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. The ABS states that imputation and weighting are the two standard treatments of non-response. (ABS Methodological News, Dec 2009)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
