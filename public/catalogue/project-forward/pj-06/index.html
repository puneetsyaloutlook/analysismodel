    PJ-06 Structured expert elicitation (IDEA protocol: investigate, discuss, estimate, aggregate)

    Elicit numeric estimates and ranges from experts before and after discussion and aggregate them mathematically, giving aggregated estimates with intervals as a draft result.

    Project forwardEvidence: UnconfirmedPrimary technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: predict, without claiming a cause

    What it does

    Elicits numeric estimates and ranges from experts before and after discussion, then aggregates mathematically. Artefact: aggregated estimates with intervals.

    When to use it

    Use when you need numeric estimates with uncertainty ranges from a small panel of experts and can ask precise, answerable questions.

    When it does not fit

    Experts are not asked for ranges, or the questions are vague.

    Result format

    The result is a set of aggregated estimates with intervals, with one row per quantity. It shows the estimates from before and after discussion and the mathematical aggregate of them.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureWrites expert-like ranges itself and aggregates them as if they came from a panel.
    Error sourcesexpert opinion; source selection
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when experts are few (imprecision), when calibration questions show overconfidence (risk of bias), or when questions are vague (indirectness).

    Related techniques

    • pairs with PJ-04 Delphi technique (real-time Delphi, mini-Delphi): a multi-round anonymous design reduces the influence of dominant panel members
    • should follow FQ-06 SMART check (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound): success criteria written beforehand make each question precise enough to answer
