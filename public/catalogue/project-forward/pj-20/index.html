    PJ-20 Wargaming and simulation exercises (policy gaming, tabletop exercise)

    Have players take the roles of competing actors in a structured scenario, giving a record of decisions and surprises as a draft result.

    Project forwardEvidence: UnconfirmedPhase: analyseClaim ceiling: describe

    What it does

    Has human players take the roles of competing actors in a structured scenario. Artefact: a record of decisions and surprises; read unexpected moves and points where the plan failed, not the outcome as a prediction.

    When to use it

    Use when you need to see how real people in the roles of competing actors respond to a plan and where it breaks down.

    When it does not fit

    Players do not represent the actors, one outcome is reported as what will happen, or adjudicators steer the result.

    Output specification

    The result is a record of the scenario, the roles played and the decisions each player made, in the order they were made. It lists the unexpected moves and the points where the plan failed, and does not present the outcome as a prediction.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failurePlays all actors itself and reports one simulated outcome as a prediction.
    Error sourcesexpert opinion; source selection
    Certainty limitsOutputs are insight, not prediction; confidence drops when players do not represent the actors (indirectness) or adjudication steers the result (risk of bias).

    Related techniques

    • pairs with CH-05 Red team analysis (red team, red cell): red team analysis supplies the adversary view for the player roles
    • should follow PJ-12 Scenario planning (intuitive logics, Shell approach): scenarios supply the starting conditions

    Evidence and tag basis

    Unconfirmed. No source on predictive accuracy was found; the usual position, not verified here, is that wargames generate insight and do not predict.

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
