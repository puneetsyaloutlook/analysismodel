    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    PJ-01Trend extrapolation and time-series forecasting (exponential smoothing, autoregressive integrated moving average models, trend impact analysis)Extend the measured history of one indicator with a stated functional form to give a central projection with prediction intervals, as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-02Reference class forecasting (outside view, base-rate forecasting) Also serves: Compare.Replace a bottom-up estimate with the distribution of outcomes in similar completed cases, giving a base-rate distribution and the case position in it as a draft result.Tested
    PJ-03Forecasting tournaments and superforecasting practiceProduce dated probability estimates for resolvable questions, score them with Brier scores and improve them by updating and aggregation, leaving a scored probability record as a draft result.Tested
    PJ-04Delphi technique (real-time Delphi, mini-Delphi)Collect anonymous expert estimates over two or more rounds with controlled feedback, giving the median and interquartile range for each round as a draft result.Documented
    PJ-05Prediction markets (information markets)Read the price of a defined contract as a probability for an event, giving a price series and a liquidity-qualified probability as a draft result.Tested
    PJ-06Structured expert elicitation (IDEA protocol: investigate, discuss, estimate, aggregate)Elicit numeric estimates and ranges from experts before and after discussion and aggregate them mathematically, giving aggregated estimates with intervals as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-07Technology S-curve analysis (logistic growth, technology substitution curve)Fit a logistic curve to an adoption or performance series to estimate a ceiling and an inflection date, as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-08Bass diffusion modelForecast the adoption path of a product or practice from external-influence and imitation parameters and a market potential, giving a cumulative adoption curve and peak timing as a draft result.Documented
    PJ-09Hype cycle (technology expectations curve)Place a technology on a five-phase expectations curve with a years-to-mainstream band, giving a placement to compare with measured attention data as a draft result.Tested
    PJ-10Trajectory analysis (technological trajectories, envelope curves; term not standard)Track a performance metric across product generations and extend the envelope of best performance, giving a trajectory chart and rate of improvement as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-11Agent-based modelling and Monte Carlo simulation (microsimulation)Simulate agents or random draws many times to give an outcome distribution with tail probabilities and the inputs that account for the spread, as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-12Scenario planning (intuitive logics, Shell approach)Build a small set of internally consistent plausible futures from critical uncertainties so that a strategy can be examined in each, giving named narrative scenarios as a draft result.Tested
    PJ-13Scenario matrix (two by two scenario axes, alternative futures analysis)Cross the two most critical and uncertain forces to give four scenario quadrants with narratives and signposts, as a draft result.Documented
    PJ-14Alternative futures archetypes (Manoa school, four generic futures)Apply a fixed set of generic futures to a topic to give a short image of the topic under each archetype, as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-15Futures wheel (implications wheel)Map first, second and third-order consequences of one change in rings, giving a radial diagram of consequences as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-16Causal layered analysisExamine a topic at four depths (litany, social and structural causes, worldview and myth) for the present and each alternative future, giving a four-row table as a draft result.Documented
    PJ-17Cross-impact analysis (cross-impact matrix)Adjust the probability of each event in a set according to how every other event raises or lowers it, giving adjusted event probabilities and an influence matrix as a draft result.Documented
    PJ-18BackcastingFix a desired end state and work backwards to the steps and conditions that connect it to the present, giving a dated pathway as a draft result.Documented
    PJ-19Policy stress-testingRun a policy or plan through defined adverse or alternative conditions, giving a table of performance by condition and the cheapest changes that remove failures as a draft comparison matrix.Documented
    PJ-20Wargaming and simulation exercises (policy gaming, tabletop exercise)Have players take the roles of competing actors in a structured scenario, giving a record of decisions and surprises as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-21Robust decision making, adaptive pathways and info-gap analysisTest many model runs or futures to find the conditions under which a strategy fails and design strategies that adapt, giving vulnerability scenarios and pathway maps with trigger points as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-22Technology roadmappingLay out how markets, products, technologies and resources are expected to evolve on a common time axis, giving a layered time chart with dependencies as a draft result.Unconfirmed
    PJ-23Horizon scanning (environmental scanning)Search systematically for early signs of change in sources outside the topic usual field, giving a signal database with source, date and rating as a collected evidence base.Documented
    PJ-24Weak signal detection (early warning signs, wild cards)Identify small ambiguous early indications that may precede a discontinuity, giving a watchlist with a strength rating over time as an appraised evidence base.Unconfirmed
    PJ-25Leading indicators and signposts (indicators of change, trigger points)Select measurable quantities that change before an outcome of interest and set thresholds beforehand, giving a table of indicators with lead time, threshold, source, frequency and owner as a draft result.Documented
