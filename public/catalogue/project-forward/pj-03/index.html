    PJ-03 Forecasting tournaments and superforecasting practice

    Produce dated probability estimates for resolvable questions, score them with Brier scores and improve them by updating and aggregation, leaving a scored probability record as a draft result.

    Project forwardEvidence: TestedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: predict, without claiming a cause

    What it does

    Produces dated probability estimates for resolvable questions, scores them with Brier scores and improves them by training, teaming, updating and aggregation. Artefact: a probability record and score; read calibration and resolution.

    When to use it

    Use when you need calibrated probabilities for questions that will resolve on a dated, observable outcome and forecasters can update over time.

    When it does not fit

    The question cannot be resolved by a dated observable outcome, the horizon is long, forecasts are stated in words only, or nothing is scored.

    Result format

    The result is a probability record with one row per question, giving the date, the probability estimate and, once resolved, the outcome and the Brier score. It reports calibration and resolution.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureStates a single probability once without dates, updates or a scoring rule, which cannot be calibrated.
    Error sourcesexpert opinion; input data
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when few questions have resolved (imprecision), when the horizon is long (indirectness), or when forecasters share information or views (risk of bias).
