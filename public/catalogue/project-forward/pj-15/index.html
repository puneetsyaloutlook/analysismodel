    PJ-15 Futures wheel (implications wheel)

    Map first, second and third-order consequences of one change in rings, giving a radial diagram of consequences as a draft result.

    Project forwardEvidence: UnconfirmedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Starts from one change and maps first, second and third-order consequences in rings. Artefact: a radial diagram; read consequences reached from more than one branch.

    When to use it

    Use when you want to explore the wider consequences of one specific change or decision before choosing how to respond.

    When it does not fit

    The central item is a vague theme, only positive or only negative consequences are listed, or the wheel is treated as a causal model with probabilities.

    Result format

    The result is a radial structure with the one change at the centre and rings of first, second and third-order consequences, each consequence linked to the one it follows from. It names the consequences that are reached from more than one branch.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilitymodel alone
    Typical model failureLists only favourable consequences, or presents the wheel as a probability-weighted causal model.
    Error sourceslanguage model; expert opinion
    Certainty limitsConfidence drops when consequences are not supported by cases of similar changes (indirectness) or when one analyst built the wheel (risk of bias).
