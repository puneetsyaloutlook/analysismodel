    PJ-08 Bass diffusion model

    Forecast the adoption path of a product or practice from external-influence and imitation parameters and a market potential, giving a cumulative adoption curve and peak timing as a draft result.

    Project forwardEvidence: DocumentedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: predict, without claiming a cause

    What it does

    Forecasts the adoption path of a new product or practice from an external-influence parameter, an imitation parameter and a market potential. Artefact: a cumulative adoption curve and peak timing.

    When to use it

    Use when you ask how quickly a new product or practice will be adopted and you have an adoption series or comparable products to estimate the parameters from.

    When it does not fit

    Market potential is guessed, parameters come from an unlike product, network effects or regulation are not represented, or an estimated parameter is negative.

    Result format

    The result is a cumulative adoption curve built from the external-influence parameter, the imitation parameter and the market potential, with each value stated. It gives the timing of the peak.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilitymodel plus code
    Typical model failureTakes parameters from an unlike product and presents the resulting peak date without stating how uncertain the market potential is.
    Error sourcesinput data; statistical assumptions; expert opinion
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when parameters are borrowed from unlike products (indirectness), market potential is guessed (imprecision), or no pre-launch accuracy evidence exists.

    Related techniques

    • pairs with PJ-07 Technology S-curve analysis (logistic growth, technology substitution curve): a logistic fit gives a comparison without the imitation assumption
    • pairs with PJ-02 Reference class forecasting (outside view, base-rate forecasting) Also serves: Compare.: base rates from comparable launches check the borrowed parameters

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. Bass (1969) reported a fit to almost all product introductions studied; pre-launch accuracy evidence was not found. (Wikipedia, Bass diffusion model)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
