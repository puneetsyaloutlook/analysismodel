    Display risk and uncertainty

    These techniques show uncertainty and risk to a reader: quantile dotplots, fan charts, icon arrays, intervals with a stated type, absolute risks in a consistent format and summary of findings tables. Step 8 chooses from them when the answer reports estimates or risks.

    9 techniques. Open one to see what it does, when it fits and what it needs from your material.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    RU-01Quantile dotplot (Kay and colleagues)Draws a probability distribution as a small number of dots, 20 in the best condition, placed at evenly spaced quantiles, so readers count dots to estimate a probability.Tested
    RU-02Hypothetical outcome plots (HOPs)Animates a sequence of random draws from the distributions, one frame per draw, so readers judge how often one quantity exceeds another.Tested
    RU-03Interval display with labelled type (error bars labelled as confidence interval, standard error or prediction interval)Shows a point estimate with an interval and states which interval it is.Tested
    RU-04Fan chart (probability bands for a forecast)Shades nested prediction intervals around a forecast path, as in the Bank of England's bands from 10 to 90 per cent.Documented
    RU-05Icon array (pictograph; isotype risk chart)Shows a risk as coloured icons in a grid of 100 or 1,000, so the reader sees the numerator against the denominator.Tested
    RU-06Absolute risk and frequency formats with consistent denominators (natural frequencies; "x in 100")States risk as absolute risk and as counts out of a fixed reference group, with the same denominator across options, instead of relative risk reductions.Tested
    RU-07Both framings with a reference class (positive and negative framing; "100 operations on people like you")Gives the outcome both with and without the event, such as deaths and survivals, for a stated reference group.Documented
    RU-08Summary of findings table (GRADE SoF table; evidence profile in summary form)Lists on one page the most important outcomes, including adverse effects, with the risk with and without the intervention, the relative effect, the number of participants and studies, and the certainty of evidence.Tested
    RU-09Uncertainty layer for official statistics (quality bands from coefficients of variation; variant projections; specific caveats)Presents uncertainty alongside each estimate through intervals with plain explanation, colour-coded quality bands, variant projections, error bars and caveats that name the bias, assumption or revision.Documented
