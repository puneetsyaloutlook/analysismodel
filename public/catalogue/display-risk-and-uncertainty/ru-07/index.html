    RU-07 Both framings with a reference class (positive and negative framing; "100 operations on people like you")

    Gives the outcome both with and without the event, such as deaths and survivals, for a stated reference group.

    Display risk and uncertaintyEvidence: DocumentedReport technique, runs in step 8

    What it does

    Gives the outcome both with and without the event, such as deaths and survivals, for a stated reference group. Artefact: paired statements or a paired chart.

    When to use it

    Use when the answer must show how uncertain an estimate or a risk is.

    When it does not fit

    Fits any single rate meant to inform a decision. No test was opened.

    Result format

    The result is paired statements or a paired chart.
