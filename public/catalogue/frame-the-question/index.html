    Frame the question

    6 techniques.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    FQ-01PICO family (population, intervention, comparison, outcome; variants PICOT, PEO, PICo, SPIDER)Splits a vague topic into named slots (population, intervention or exposure, comparison, outcome) and produces a one-sentence structured question and a slot table.Documented
    FQ-02Decision-first framing (decision-focused or value-focused question design)Starts from the decision to be made and works back to a list of questions, each tagged with the option it could change.Unconfirmed
    FQ-03Definitional analysis (explication, concept clarification, conceptual analysis)Replaces ambiguous key terms with precise working definitions and produces a glossary with borderline cases.Documented
    FQ-04Reframing and issue redefinition (changing the level of analysis or baseline; five-step issue redefinition)Restates the question at another level, with another baseline, or by rephrasing, broadening, narrowing and reversing it, and produces answers side by side.Practice
    FQ-05Multiple mental models (applying explicit models from several fields to one topic)Reads one topic through several explicit models from different fields and produces one reading per model.Practice
    FQ-06SMART check (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound)Checks that an objective or question names a scope, a measure, a feasible target and a time limit, and produces the draft rewritten with each criterion answered.Practice
