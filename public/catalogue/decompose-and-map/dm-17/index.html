    DM-17 Process mapping (flowcharts, swimlane diagrams, value stream maps)

    Records the sequence of steps, inputs, outputs and responsible roles in a process and produces a flow diagram showing handoffs, delays and rework loops.

    Decompose and mapEvidence: UnconfirmedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Records the sequence of steps, inputs, outputs and responsible roles in a process. Artefact: a flow diagram; read handoffs, delays and rework loops.

    When to use it

    Use when you need to see how work actually moves through a process and where it is delayed or repeated.

    When it does not fit

    The real work is not sequential or varies widely by case.

    Result format

    The result is a flow diagram of the process showing each step in sequence with its inputs, outputs and responsible role. It names the handoffs, delays and rework loops.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureDraws the intended process from policy documents and presents it as the actual process.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement; language model
    Certainty limitsDocuments describe the intended steps and not the actual steps; observation or interviews are needed for indirectness to be low.
