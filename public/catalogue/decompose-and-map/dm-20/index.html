    DM-20 Stock and flow modelling (system dynamics, including simulation) Also serves: Project forward.

    Represents accumulations that change only through inflows and outflows and, when simulated, produces a diagram and simulated time paths.

    Decompose and mapEvidence: DocumentedPhase: analyseClaim ceiling: predict without implying cause

    What it does

    Represents accumulations that change only through inflows and outflows, and when simulated produces time paths. Artefact: a diagram and simulated paths; read accumulation, durations and sensitivity to parameters, not point values.

    When to use it

    Use when the question concerns a quantity that builds up or runs down over time and you want to see durations and sensitivity to assumptions.

    When it does not fit

    The quantity cannot be framed as an accumulation, flow data are unavailable, the model cannot reproduce the history it should explain, or the conclusion rests on one untested parameter.

    Output specification

    The result is a diagram of stocks, inflows and outflows with the simulated time path of each stock. It reports accumulation, durations and sensitivity to parameters, and does not rest on point values.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureCalculates simulated paths in text instead of code and reports point values from parameters that were guessed.
    Error sourcesinput data; statistical assumptions; expert opinion; language model
    Certainty limitsDelays and feedback strengths often rest on expert judgement; simulation accuracy is contested and paths are conditional on parameters, which lowers certainty by imprecision and risk of bias.

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. The diagram form is documented (Wikipedia, Stock and flow). Simulation accuracy is contested: a 2008 working paper by Turner reportedly found that observed data tracked the Limits to Growth standard run, but it was not opened.

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
