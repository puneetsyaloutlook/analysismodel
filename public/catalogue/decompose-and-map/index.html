    Decompose and map

    21 techniques.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    DM-01MECE decomposition and issue tree (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive; logic tree, problem tree; related: work breakdown structure)Divides a topic into sub-parts that do not overlap and together cover the whole, repeated at each level, and produces a tree.Practice
    DM-02Hypothesis tree (hypothesis-driven decomposition, claim decomposition)Splits a claim into the sub-claims that must all be true for it to be true and produces a tree of claims that goes into a claims register.Unconfirmed
    DM-03Morphological analysis (Zwicky box, general morphological analysis) Also serves: Project forward.Lists the parameters of a topic and the possible values of each, removes value pairs judged inconsistent, and produces a grid and a reduced set of consistent configurations.Documented
    DM-04Value chain analysis (Porter)Divides the activities of one business unit into five primary and four support activities and produces a value chain showing where cost and differentiation concentrate.Documented
    DM-05Taxonomy building (classification scheme, typology, controlled vocabulary)Builds a scheme of classes from observable characteristics, assigns items to the classes and produces a hierarchy and a draft codebook.Documented
    DM-06Affinity diagram (KJ method)Groups many recorded items, such as survey comments or interview quotes, into clusters that form from the relationships among the items, and produces labelled clusters.Documented
    DM-07Lens application (sorting claims by a classification lens, for example input versus outcome)Sorts a set of claims or measures by chosen categories, such as input or outcome, and produces a table of claims by category.Unconfirmed
    DM-08Two by two matrix (quadrant analysis) Also serves: Assess actors, options and risks; Project forward.Places items on two chosen dimensions so that each falls into one of four cells and produces a quadrant table.Unconfirmed
    DM-09PESTLE (political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental; the shorter PEST omits legal and environmental; outside-in thinking)Sorts external factors into political, economic, social, technological, legal and environmental categories and produces a categorised list.Documented
    DM-10SWOT (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats)Sorts factors into internal strengths and weaknesses and external opportunities and threats and produces a two by two matrix.Documented
    DM-11Porter's Five Forces (industry structure analysis)Rates rivalry, new entrants, substitutes, buyer power and supplier power and produces a five-force diagram with each force rated.Documented
    DM-12Cynefin frameworkClassifies a situation, or each part of it, as clear, complicated, complex, chaotic or confusion by the relation of cause and effect and produces a domain assignment.Documented
    DM-13System archetypes (limits to growth, shifting the burden, fixes that fail, and others)Matches an observed behaviour pattern to a recurring feedback structure and produces a named pattern with the loop structure to change.Documented
    DM-14Leverage points (Meadows' twelve places to intervene in a system)Ranks types of intervention in a system from least to most effective and produces an ordered list showing the level at which a proposed intervention acts.Documented
    DM-15Wardley mappingPositions the components of a service by visibility to the user and by evolution stage, with dependency lines, and produces a two-axis map.Practice
    DM-16Levels-of-analysis models (Marr's three levels, micro, meso and macro levels)Examines a topic at several distinct levels, each answering a different kind of question, and produces findings organised by level.Documented
    DM-17Process mapping (flowcharts, swimlane diagrams, value stream maps)Records the sequence of steps, inputs, outputs and responsible roles in a process and produces a flow diagram showing handoffs, delays and rework loops.Unconfirmed
    DM-18Design structure matrix (dependency structure matrix)Records which element depends on or supplies which in a square matrix, reorders it to reveal clusters or sequence, and produces a reordered matrix.Documented
    DM-19Causal loop diagram Also serves: Trace cause and test claims.Draws variables and signed causal links so that reinforcing and balancing loops and delays can be identified, and produces a diagram with loop labels.Documented
    DM-20Stock and flow modelling (system dynamics, including simulation) Also serves: Project forward.Represents accumulations that change only through inflows and outflows and, when simulated, produces a diagram and simulated time paths.Documented
    DM-21Social network analysis (sociogram, network analysis) Also serves: Assess actors, options and risks.Represents actors as nodes and relationships as ties, measures structure with graph metrics, and produces a sociogram, an entity table and metrics.Documented
