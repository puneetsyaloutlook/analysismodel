    DM-12 Cynefin framework

    Classifies a situation, or each part of it, as clear, complicated, complex, chaotic or confusion by the relation of cause and effect and produces a domain assignment.

    Decompose and mapEvidence: DocumentedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Classifies a situation, or each part of it, as clear, complicated, complex, chaotic or confusion by the relation of cause and effect, which fixes the suitable response. Artefact: a domain assignment; read the share of parts in each domain.

    When to use it

    Use when you need to decide what kind of response a situation calls for and cause and effect are in question.

    When it does not fit

    People disagree about which domain a part belongs to, or the classification changes each time it is made.

    Result format

    The result assigns the situation, or each part of it, to one domain (clear, complicated, complex, chaotic or confusion), with the cause and effect relation that justifies the assignment and the response that domain suits. It gives the share of parts in each domain.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureAssigns a domain from the surface features of the description and states a confident classification where the causal judgement is contestable.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement; language model
    Certainty limitsThe classification depends on a contestable causal judgement; labels changed repeatedly over time and there is no controlled testing.
