    Recommend and tailor to the audience

    These techniques move from conclusions to action: an evidence to decision framework, labels for the strength of a recommendation, an audience analysis, a statement of implications and actionable messages. Step 8 chooses from them only when the request asks for recommendations or names an audience.

    10 techniques. Open one to see what it does, when it fits and what it needs from your material.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    RA-01Evidence to decision framework (GRADE EtD (evidence to decision); DECIDE)Moves from evidence to a recommendation through fixed criteria: priority of the problem, desirable and undesirable effects, certainty, values, balance of effects, resource use, acceptability, feasibility and, for population decisions, equity.Documented
    RA-02Recommendation strength labelling (strong and weak or conditional recommendations; good practice statements)Labels each recommendation with its direction and strength alongside the certainty of evidence, with good practice statements as a separate category.Tested
    RA-03Cabinet business forms (Australian Government Cabinet Handbook, 16th edition: Cabinet Submission, Preliminary Discussion Submission, Cabinet Memorandum)Sets the format for bringing a matter to Cabinet.Documented
    RA-04Impact analysis with a preferred option (Australian Government seven impact analysis questions)Answers seven questions: the policy problem and available data, the objective and its success measure, the options, the net benefit of each, the consultation undertaken, the best option and its implementation, and evaluation.Documented
    RA-05Five case model (UK Green Book business case)Presents a proposal in strategic, economic, commercial, financial and management cases, with the preferred option taken from the economic case.Unconfirmed
    RA-06Audience analysis and use plan (Magenta Book use and dissemination plan; tailored messaging; reader analysis)Names each group that will use the answer, what information it needs, at which decision point and for what purpose, and tailors an output to each, such as a one-page summary, an infographic or a seminar.Documented
    RA-07Implications statement ("so what"; ICD 203 customer relevance and implications)States what a finding means for the reader's decision, beyond what the finding is.Unconfirmed
    RA-08Actionable messages (Lavis and colleagues 2003)Short statements of what a named audience can do, drawn from a body of research rather than one study, framed by what is transferred, to whom, by whom, how and with what effect.Unconfirmed
    RA-09Knowledge brokering (knowledge broker role)A person or unit works between researchers and decision makers to find, interpret and adapt evidence for the decision makers' use.Tested
    RA-10Knowledge to action framework (Graham and colleagues 2006)A process model with a knowledge creation funnel and an action cycle that includes adapting knowledge to the local context, assessing barriers and monitoring use.Unconfirmed
