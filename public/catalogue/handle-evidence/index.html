    Handle evidence

    29 techniques.

    IDTechniquePurposeEvidence status
    HE1-01Admiralty system (source reliability and information credibility grading)Assigns each report a two-character grade for source reliability (A to F) and information credibility (1 to 6) and produces an appraised evidence base.Documented
    HE1-02Quality of information checkRates the completeness, accuracy and corroboration of each source and produces a source database with a statement of what is known and unknown.Documented
    HE1-03CRAAP test (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose)Screens one source against five criteria (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose) and produces a checklist judgement for the evidence base.Documented
    HE1-04SIFT (stop, investigate the source, find better coverage, trace claims to the original; claim verification and fact-checking)Moves from a claim to its publisher, to other coverage and to the original source, and produces a short credibility judgement with the original context located.Practice
    HE1-05Lateral readingLeaves the page to read about the publisher in other sources and produces a judgement of publisher credibility.Unconfirmed
    HE1-06Triangulation (source, method, investigator and theory triangulation; corroboration)Compares independent lines of evidence on one question and produces a convergence table of agreement, disagreement and reasons.Unconfirmed
    HE1-07Evidence hierarchies and certainty grading (study design levels, risk-of-bias assessment, Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation or GRADE)Ranks study designs and rates confidence in a body of evidence from high to very low, and produces a certainty rating with reasons for downgrading.Documented
    HE1-08Systematic reviewCollects all eligible studies by a pre-specified, reproducible search, appraises and synthesises them, and produces a screening log, an evidence table and a synthesis.Documented
    HE1-09Rapid reviewRuns a systematic review with streamlined steps to meet a deadline and produces a narrative synthesis that states the shortcuts taken.Documented
    HE1-10Scoping review (evidence mapping)Maps the extent, types and gaps of evidence on a broad topic without judging the strength of findings, and produces a charted table.Unconfirmed
    HE1-11Meta-analysisCombines effect estimates from separate studies into a weighted average with a confidence interval and produces a forest plot, a pooled effect and heterogeneity statistics.Documented
    HE1-12Narrative, thematic and qualitative evidence synthesis (including meta-ethnography)Combines findings across studies in words or themes when pooling numbers is not appropriate, and produces a structured narrative or theme set linked back to studies.Documented
    HE1-13Reflexive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke)Identifies and interprets patterns of meaning across qualitative data in six phases and produces themes with supporting extracts.Documented
    HE1-14Framework analysis (Framework Method)Sorts coded qualitative data into a case by theme matrix and produces the matrix for reading across cases and themes.Documented
    HE1-15Content analysis (conventional, directed and summative)Codes text into categories, then counts or interprets category use, and produces category frequencies or a category structure.Unconfirmed
    HE1-16Discourse analysis (critical discourse analysis)Examines how language constructs topics and positions actors and produces an interpretive account of framing checked against quoted passages.Unconfirmed
    HE1-17Grounded theory coding (open, axial and selective coding; constant comparison)Builds categories and a theory from data through open, axial and selective coding with constant comparison and produces codes, memos and a theory.Unconfirmed
    HE1-18Document and policy analysisReads documents systematically to extract facts, positions, changes or stated reasons using their provenance and purpose, and produces an extraction table or chronology.Unconfirmed
    HE1-19Bibliometric analysis (citation analysis, science mapping, co-citation, co-word analysis)Counts and maps publications, authors and citations to show the structure and growth of a field and produces performance tables and network maps.Unconfirmed
    HE1-20Trend and time-series decomposition Also serves: Project forward.Separates a series into trend, seasonal and irregular parts and produces plots and a fitted trend.Unconfirmed
    HE1-21Distribution, outlier and base-rate checksExamines the shape, extremes and baseline frequency of data before a figure is interpreted and produces histograms, summary statistics, flagged outliers and a base rate.Unconfirmed
    HE-01Grey literature appraisal checklist (AACODS (Authority, Accuracy, Coverage, Objectivity, Date, Significance))Scores a non-peer-reviewed item on six criteria (authority, accuracy, coverage, objectivity, date, significance) and produces a completed checklist.Documented
    HE-02Critical appraisal checklist by study design (CASP (Critical Appraisal Skills Programme) checklists; JBI (Joanna Briggs Institute) checklists)Answers design-specific questions on validity, results and relevance for one study and produces a completed checklist for the evidence base.Documented
    HE-03Appraisal of a systematic review (AMSTAR 2 (A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews 2); ROBIS (Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews))Rates confidence in a systematic review with AMSTAR 2 (high to critically low) or concern in four domains with ROBIS, and produces a rating or domain judgements.Documented
    HE-04Survey disclosure completeness check (AAPOR (American Association for Public Opinion Research) disclosure elements)Tests whether a survey report states the sponsor, question wording, population, sampling frame, mode, dates, sample size, margin of error and weighting, and produces a list of disclosed and missing items.Documented
    HE-05Margin of error and credibility interval checkChecks that a reported margin of error matches the sampling method and produces a judgement on whether the figure is valid.Documented
    HE-06Opt-in panel respondent quality check (bogus respondent screen)Looks for implausible answers that show careless or fraudulent respondents inflating rare responses in an opt-in survey and produces a judgement of inflation risk.Documented
    HE-07Overview of reviews (umbrella review; tertiary review)Uses systematic reviews as the unit of search, inclusion and analysis and produces a summary of reviews with a quality rating per review and an overlap map.Documented
    HE-08Living systematic reviewKeeps a systematic review continuously updated as new studies appear and produces a versioned evidence summary and an update log.Documented
