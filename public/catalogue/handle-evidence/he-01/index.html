    HE-01 Grey literature appraisal checklist (AACODS (Authority, Accuracy, Coverage, Objectivity, Date, Significance))

    Scores a non-peer-reviewed item on six criteria (authority, accuracy, coverage, objectivity, date, significance) and produces a completed checklist.

    Handle evidenceEvidence: DocumentedPreparation technique, runs in step 5.2Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Scores a non-peer-reviewed item on six criteria, each answered yes, no or unclear. Artefact: a completed checklist. Read the Objectivity and Accuracy answers for unstated bias and unsupported claims.

    When to use it

    Use when you must decide whether to rely on a government, organisation or consultancy report that has no design-specific checklist.

    When it does not fit

    Fits an item with no design-specific checklist. Misfit: the item is a study whose design has its own checklist. The checklist has no sponsorship item, so the analyst must record funder and author affiliations at the Authority and Objectivity steps.

    Result format

    The result is a completed checklist with one row per criterion (authority, accuracy, coverage, objectivity, date, significance), each answered yes, no or unclear. It states what the objectivity and accuracy answers show about unstated bias and unsupported claims.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilitymodel alone
    Typical model failureAnswers yes on authority and objectivity from the report's own claims, and does not record funder or affiliation.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement; language model
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when the checklist is read as a score (it has no scoring rule), when the funder is unknown, and when one assessor answers every item (risk of bias).

    Related techniques

    • substitutes for HE1-03 CRAAP test (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose): when document_genre is government or agency report or organisation report and a structured checklist is wanted
    • pairs with CM-01 Sponsor versus non-sponsor comparison (meta-epidemiological sponsorship analysis): funder and affiliation records support a comparison of sponsored and non-sponsored findings

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. Written by Tyndall at Flinders University in 2010, with no scoring rule or inclusion threshold. No study of its validity or reliability was found. (AACODS checklist)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
