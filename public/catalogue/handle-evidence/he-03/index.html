    HE-03 Appraisal of a systematic review (AMSTAR 2 (A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews 2); ROBIS (Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews))

    Rates confidence in a systematic review with AMSTAR 2 (high to critically low) or concern in four domains with ROBIS, and produces a rating or domain judgements.

    Handle evidenceEvidence: DocumentedPreparation technique, runs in step 5.2Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    AMSTAR 2 rates confidence in a review from 16 items and seven critical domains as high, moderate, low or critically low. ROBIS rates concern in four domains and gives no summary score. Artefact: a rating or domain judgements. Read which critical items failed or which domains raise concern.

    When to use it

    Use when you need to decide whether a published systematic review can be relied on.

    When it does not fit

    Fits healthcare reviews of interventions. Misfit: a consultancy review that is not systematic. In one ROBIS pilot two reviewers agreed on 26 of 40 domain ratings (65 per cent). The Cochrane Handbook recommends neither instrument over the other.

    Result format

    The result is a confidence rating of high, moderate, low or critically low for the review, or a judgement of concern for each of the four domains, depending on the tool used. It names the critical items that failed or the domains that raise concern.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureRates critical items as met without reading the methods, and gives a single score for ROBIS, which has none.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops with low inter-assessor agreement, a single assessor, and application outside healthcare (indirectness).

    Related techniques

    • pairs with HE-07 Overview of reviews (umbrella review; tertiary review): each review in an overview needs this appraisal
    • pairs with HE1-07 Evidence hierarchies and certainty grading (study design levels, risk-of-bias assessment, Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation or GRADE): the review rating feeds certainty of a body of evidence

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. ROBIS is not validated outside healthcare. (AMSTAR 2, BMJ 2017; ROBIS, J Clin Epidemiol; Cochrane Handbook chapter V)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
