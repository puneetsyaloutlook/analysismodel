    HE1-04 SIFT (stop, investigate the source, find better coverage, trace claims to the original; claim verification and fact-checking)

    Moves from a claim to its publisher, to other coverage and to the original source, and produces a short credibility judgement with the original context located.

    Handle evidenceEvidence: PracticePhase: verify and prepareClaim ceiling: describe

    What it does

    Moves a reader off the page to find who published a claim, what others say, and where it began. Artefact: a short judgement and a located original context; read whether the original differs from the version met.

    When to use it

    Use when you meet a claim online and need to know who is behind it, what others say and where it began.

    When it does not fit

    The claim is technical and the checker cannot judge the expertise involved.

    Output specification

    The result is a short judgement of the claim's source that names who published it, what other sources say about it, and where the claim began. It states the located original context and says whether the original differs from the version first met.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilitymodel alone
    Typical model failureReports a trace to an original that it did not find, or accepts the first search result as independent coverage.
    Error sourcesinput data; language model; source selection
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when the original cannot be found, when coverage found is derived from one origin, or when search results are limited (reporting bias).

    Related techniques

    • pairs with HE1-05 Lateral reading: lateral reading is the step that investigates the publisher
    • pairs with TC-04 Claim and citation verification against source text (fact verification; claim checking; citation support check; quotation accuracy check): SIFT finds the original and claim verification checks the claim against its text
