    HE-02 Critical appraisal checklist by study design (CASP (Critical Appraisal Skills Programme) checklists; JBI (Joanna Briggs Institute) checklists)

    Answers design-specific questions on validity, results and relevance for one study and produces a completed checklist for the evidence base.

    Handle evidenceEvidence: DocumentedPreparation technique, runs in step 5.2Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Answers design-specific questions on validity, results and relevance. JBI lists 15 checklists, including prevalence, analytical cross-sectional and qualitative studies and three for textual evidence (expert opinion, narrative, policy). Artefact: item answers. Read the validity items first.

    When to use it

    Use when you need to judge how far to trust a single study, such as a randomised trial, a cohort study, a qualitative study or an expert opinion paper, and its design has a checklist.

    When it does not fit

    Choose the checklist by the design the study used. Neither source gives a scoring rule, and the items in the JBI textual evidence checklists were not read.

    Result format

    The result is a list of answers to the checklist questions that fit the study design, with the validity items listed first and then the results and relevance items. Each answer is given beside its item.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failurePicks the wrong checklist for the design, or answers items from the abstract without the methods section.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when items are answered from reports that omit methods, when one appraiser works alone, and when the checklist is turned into a score (risk of bias).

    Related techniques

    • pairs with HE1-07 Evidence hierarchies and certainty grading (study design levels, risk-of-bias assessment, Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation or GRADE): item answers feed the certainty rating
    • should follow HE1-08 Systematic review: appraisal applies to the included studies of a review

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. CASP describes its checklists as a guide and not a replacement for judgement. JBI gives no scoring rule on the page read. (JBI critical appraisal tools; CASP knowledge hub)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
