    CH-11 Steelmanning Also serves: Compare.

    Builds the strongest version of a position the analyst disagrees with, responds to it, and produces a strengthened opposing argument and a response.

    ChallengeEvidence: PracticeChallenge technique, runs in step 6Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Builds the strongest version of a position the analyst disagrees with, then responds to it. Artefact: a strengthened opposing argument and a response, which tests the analyst's own position.

    When to use it

    Use when you want to test your own position against the best form of the opposing argument and not a weak version.

    When it does not fit

    The strengthened version is no longer recognisable as the opponent's position, or adds claims the opponent rejects.

    Result format

    The result has two parts: the opposing position restated in its strongest form, and a response to that restated version. It must keep the restated position recognisable to its holder and end with a statement of what the response shows about the analyst's own position.
