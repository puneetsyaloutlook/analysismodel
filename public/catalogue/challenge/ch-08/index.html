    CH-08 Premortem (prospective hindsight, inversion) Also serves: Project forward.

    Asks a team to assume the plan has already failed and to write the reasons, and produces a grouped list of failure reasons and draft assumptions.

    ChallengeEvidence: PracticeChallenge technique, runs in step 6Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Asks a team to assume the plan has already failed and to write the reasons. Artefact: a ranked or grouped list of failure reasons; read clusters and reasons raised only by sceptical members.

    When to use it

    Use when a plan is about to be adopted and you want the team to say why it might fail while it can still change.

    When it does not fit

    It is run after the decision has been taken, or reasons are not converted into plan changes or monitored indicators.

    Result format

    The result is a ranked or grouped list of reasons the plan failed, written as if the failure had already happened, with the assumptions behind them in an assumptions list. It names the clusters of reasons and the reasons raised only by sceptical members.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureProduces a generic failure list from general knowledge in place of the views of the team members, including the sceptics.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement; language model
    Certainty limitsDescribed by Klein in 2007; the commonly quoted effect figure is unconfirmed and no evidence of improved outcomes was found.

    Related techniques

    • pairs with AO-16 Failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA): Failure mode analysis rates the failure reasons by effect and likelihood
    • pairs with AO-15 Risk matrix (likelihood and consequence matrix, risk heat map): A risk matrix prioritises the reasons found

    Evidence and tag basis

    Practice. Described by Klein in 2007; the commonly quoted effect figure from Mitchell, Russo and Pennington (1989) is unconfirmed, and no evidence of improved outcomes was found. (Klein, Performing a project premortem, Harvard Business Review; Wikipedia, Pre-mortem)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
