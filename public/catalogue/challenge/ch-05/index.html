    CH-05 Red team analysis (red team, red cell)

    Models how an adversary or other actor would decide by reasoning in that actor first-person voice, or examines a plan from outside the planning team, and produces first-person memos predicting decisions.

    ChallengeEvidence: DocumentedChallenge technique, runs in step 6Strongest statement: predict, without claiming a cause

    What it does

    Models how an adversary or other actor would decide by reasoning in that actor's first-person voice (CIA), or examines a plan from outside the planning team (UK). Artefact: first-person memos predicting decisions; read decisions that differ from the home team's expectation.

    When to use it

    Use when you need to anticipate how another actor will decide or want an outside team to examine a plan.

    When it does not fit

    The analysts cannot escape their own mindsets, or an external team is misused to attack the plan.

    Result format

    The result is a set of first-person memos, written in the voice of the adversary or other actor, that predict its decisions. It names the decisions that differ from the home team's expectation.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureWrites the other actor decisions in the home team terms and values, and so mirrors its own expectation.
    Error sourcesinput data; expert opinion; language model
    Certainty limitsThe CIA and UK sources use the same name for different purposes and give no effectiveness evidence; predictions rest on expert knowledge of the actor and carry imprecision.

    Related techniques

    • pairs with PJ-20 Wargaming and simulation exercises (policy gaming, tabletop exercise): A wargame tests the predicted decisions through play
    • pairs with CH-03 Devil's advocacy: Devil advocacy challenges the home view and red team analysis models the other actor

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. The two sources use the same name for different purposes: the UK red cell is closest to the CIA red team analysis. No effectiveness evidence found. (CIA Tradecraft Primer (2009); UK Red Teaming Handbook)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
