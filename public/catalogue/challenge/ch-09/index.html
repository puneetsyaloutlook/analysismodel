    CH-09 Structured self-critique

    Asks about sources of uncertainty, evidence, gaps, critical assumptions and alternative hypotheses, and produces a written critique of one own analysis.

    ChallengeEvidence: DocumentedChallenge technique, runs in step 6Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Asks about sources of uncertainty, evidence, gaps, critical assumptions and alternative hypotheses. Artefact: a written critique of one's own analysis.

    When to use it

    Use when you have drafted an analysis and want to find its gaps and weak assumptions before it is reviewed by others.

    When it does not fit

    It is applied dishonestly; the handbook says it is only as good as the honesty with which it is applied.

    Result format

    The result is a written critique of the analyst's own analysis that covers the sources of uncertainty, the evidence, the gaps, the critical assumptions and the alternative hypotheses. Each of these headings has its own section.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilitymodel alone
    Typical model failureReports that the analysis is sound and lists minor gaps, because the model reviews its own output with the same biases.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement; language model
    Certainty limitsA self-critique is not independent of the analysis it reviews; the result carries risk of bias and the technique overlaps with other checks.
