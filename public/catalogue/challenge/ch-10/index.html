    CH-10 Rapoport's rules (fair restatement of an opposing position) Also serves: Compare.

    Requires a fair restatement of the opposing position, a list of points of agreement and of what was learned before any rebuttal, and produces a restatement and an agreement list.

    ChallengeEvidence: DocumentedChallenge technique, runs in step 6Strongest statement: describe

    What it does

    Requires the analyst to restate the opposing position fairly, list points of agreement and what was learned, and only then rebut. Artefact: a restatement and an agreement list; the remaining disagreements are the real ones.

    When to use it

    Use when two sides talk past each other and you want to find the disagreements that remain once each side has been stated fairly.

    When it does not fit

    The opponent would not accept the restatement as well expressed. It is a debate procedure and does not show which position is correct (inference).

    Result format

    The result has a fair restatement of the opposing position, a list of the points of agreement and a statement of what was learned, all placed before any rebuttal. It ends with the remaining disagreements, which are the real ones.
