    FQ-01PICO family (population, intervention, comparison, outcome; variants PICOT, PEO, PICo, SPIDER)Frame the questionSplits a vague topic into named slots (population, intervention or exposure, comparison, outcome) and produces a one-sentence structured question and a slot table.
    FQ-02Decision-first framing (decision-focused or value-focused question design)Frame the questionStarts from the decision to be made and works back to a list of questions, each tagged with the option it could change.
    FQ-03Definitional analysis (explication, concept clarification, conceptual analysis)Frame the questionReplaces ambiguous key terms with precise working definitions and produces a glossary with borderline cases.
    FQ-04Reframing and issue redefinition (changing the level of analysis or baseline; five-step issue redefinition)Frame the questionRestates the question at another level, with another baseline, or by rephrasing, broadening, narrowing and reversing it, and produces answers side by side.
    FQ-05Multiple mental models (applying explicit models from several fields to one topic)Frame the questionReads one topic through several explicit models from different fields and produces one reading per model.
    FQ-06SMART check (specific, measurable, achievable, relevant, time-bound)Frame the questionChecks that an objective or question names a scope, a measure, a feasible target and a time limit, and produces the draft rewritten with each criterion answered.
    DM-01MECE decomposition and issue tree (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive; logic tree, problem tree; related: work breakdown structure)Decompose and mapDivides a topic into sub-parts that do not overlap and together cover the whole, repeated at each level, and produces a tree.
    DM-02Hypothesis tree (hypothesis-driven decomposition, claim decomposition)Decompose and mapSplits a claim into the sub-claims that must all be true for it to be true and produces a tree of claims that goes into a claims register.
    DM-03Morphological analysis (Zwicky box, general morphological analysis) Also serves: Project forward.Decompose and mapLists the parameters of a topic and the possible values of each, removes value pairs judged inconsistent, and produces a grid and a reduced set of consistent configurations.
    DM-04Value chain analysis (Porter)Decompose and mapDivides the activities of one business unit into five primary and four support activities and produces a value chain showing where cost and differentiation concentrate.
    DM-05Taxonomy building (classification scheme, typology, controlled vocabulary)Decompose and mapBuilds a scheme of classes from observable characteristics, assigns items to the classes and produces a hierarchy and a draft codebook.
    DM-06Affinity diagram (KJ method)Decompose and mapGroups many recorded items, such as survey comments or interview quotes, into clusters that form from the relationships among the items, and produces labelled clusters.
    DM-07Lens application (sorting claims by a classification lens, for example input versus outcome)Decompose and mapSorts a set of claims or measures by chosen categories, such as input or outcome, and produces a table of claims by category.
    DM-08Two by two matrix (quadrant analysis) Also serves: Assess actors, options and risks; Project forward.Decompose and mapPlaces items on two chosen dimensions so that each falls into one of four cells and produces a quadrant table.
    DM-09PESTLE (political, economic, social, technological, legal, environmental; the shorter PEST omits legal and environmental; outside-in thinking)Decompose and mapSorts external factors into political, economic, social, technological, legal and environmental categories and produces a categorised list.
    DM-10SWOT (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats)Decompose and mapSorts factors into internal strengths and weaknesses and external opportunities and threats and produces a two by two matrix.
    DM-11Porter's Five Forces (industry structure analysis)Decompose and mapRates rivalry, new entrants, substitutes, buyer power and supplier power and produces a five-force diagram with each force rated.
    DM-12Cynefin frameworkDecompose and mapClassifies a situation, or each part of it, as clear, complicated, complex, chaotic or confusion by the relation of cause and effect and produces a domain assignment.
    DM-13System archetypes (limits to growth, shifting the burden, fixes that fail, and others)Decompose and mapMatches an observed behaviour pattern to a recurring feedback structure and produces a named pattern with the loop structure to change.
    DM-14Leverage points (Meadows' twelve places to intervene in a system)Decompose and mapRanks types of intervention in a system from least to most effective and produces an ordered list showing the level at which a proposed intervention acts.
    DM-15Wardley mappingDecompose and mapPositions the components of a service by visibility to the user and by evolution stage, with dependency lines, and produces a two-axis map.
    DM-16Levels-of-analysis models (Marr's three levels, micro, meso and macro levels)Decompose and mapExamines a topic at several distinct levels, each answering a different kind of question, and produces findings organised by level.
    DM-17Process mapping (flowcharts, swimlane diagrams, value stream maps)Decompose and mapRecords the sequence of steps, inputs, outputs and responsible roles in a process and produces a flow diagram showing handoffs, delays and rework loops.
    DM-18Design structure matrix (dependency structure matrix)Decompose and mapRecords which element depends on or supplies which in a square matrix, reorders it to reveal clusters or sequence, and produces a reordered matrix.
    DM-19Causal loop diagram Also serves: Trace cause and test claims.Decompose and mapDraws variables and signed causal links so that reinforcing and balancing loops and delays can be identified, and produces a diagram with loop labels.
    DM-20Stock and flow modelling (system dynamics, including simulation) Also serves: Project forward.Decompose and mapRepresents accumulations that change only through inflows and outflows and, when simulated, produces a diagram and simulated time paths.
    DM-21Social network analysis (sociogram, network analysis) Also serves: Assess actors, options and risks.Decompose and mapRepresents actors as nodes and relationships as ties, measures structure with graph metrics, and produces a sociogram, an entity table and metrics.
    CM1-01Historical analogy and parallel analysis (likenesses and differences, analogical explanation)ComparePlaces the present topic beside a past case and rates each likeness and difference, producing a likenesses and differences table that shows where a lesson from the past case may and may not be applied.
    CM1-02Mill's method of agreement and most different systems design (MDSD)CompareLooks across dissimilar cases that share an outcome for the factor present in all of them, producing a table of candidate factors by case in which the common factor is the candidate cause.
    CM1-03Mill's method of difference and most similar systems design (MSSD; joint method of agreement and difference)CompareLooks for the factor present where an outcome occurs and absent in an otherwise similar case where it does not, producing a short list of the remaining differences between matched cases.
    CM1-04Concomitant variation and method of residues (Mill)CompareInfers a link when a factor varies with the effect (concomitant variation) or attributes the unexplained part of an effect to the remaining circumstances (residues), producing a co-movement pattern or a residual.
    CM1-05Comparative case study (structured focused comparison, small-N comparison)CompareExamines a small number of cases with the same questions for each, producing a case by case matrix that shows consistent patterns and deviant cases.
    CM1-06Case selection techniques (extreme, deviant, diverse, index, most similar)CompareChooses which cases to compare by their position on a variable distribution or on a model prediction, producing a chosen case set with the rule used.
    CM1-07Qualitative comparative analysis (QCA; crisp-set, fuzzy-set and multi-value variants)CompareUses set theory and Boolean algebra to find combinations of conditions that are necessary or sufficient for an outcome across a medium number of cases, producing a truth table and minimal solution formulas with consistency and coverage scores.
    CM1-08Cross-case synthesis (replication logic)CompareCombines completed within-case write-ups into one cross-case matrix, producing a matrix that shows repeating patterns and diverging cases.
    CM1-09Contrast with a structural opposite (term from earlier work)CompareSets the topic beside a case or position that differs from it on a stated defining structure, producing a table of contrasting features that shows what each assumes or lacks.
    CM1-10Cross-sectional and longitudinal comparison (cohort and panel comparison; cross-cycle comparison)CompareCompares different units at one time (cross-sectional) or the same units repeatedly (longitudinal), producing group differences at one time or change paths over time.
    CM1-11Before and after comparison (pre-post design)CompareCompares an outcome for the same unit before and after an event, producing a change estimate.
    CM1-12Benchmarking and peer comparison (best-practice comparison, competitor analysis)CompareCompares an organisation processes and metrics with those of peers or leaders, producing a gap table of own against benchmark performance.
    CM1-13Forecaster and source comparison (forecast scoring, aggregation, track record comparison) Also serves: Project forward.CompareScores a forecast against those of other forecasters on resolved outcomes and aggregates them, producing an accuracy ranking and an aggregate forecast.
    CM1-14Absence analysis (argument from silence, the dog that did not bark) Also serves: Handle evidence.CompareDraws a conclusion from an item missing from a document or comparison set where it would be expected if a claim were true, producing a list of expected but absent items.
    CM1-15Gap analysisCompareCompares current with desired performance, producing a prioritised list of gaps and the means to close each.
    CM1-16Maturity model and capability assessment (Capability Maturity Model Integration, CMMI)CompareRates processes against staged levels, producing a maturity level or capability profile that shows where an organisation stands and what to improve.
    CM-01Sponsor versus non-sponsor comparison (meta-epidemiological sponsorship analysis)CompareCompares the share of favourable results and conclusions between sponsored and independently funded reports on one topic, producing a risk ratio or odds ratio.
    TC1-01Five whysTrace cause and test claimsAsks why repeatedly about one stated problem and records a single chain of causes, leaving a candidate root cause as a result.
    TC1-02Fishbone diagram (Ishikawa diagram, cause and effect diagram)Trace cause and test claimsGroups brainstormed causes of one problem under category branches and produces a fishbone diagram with the problem as its head.
    TC1-03Fault tree analysis Also serves: Assess actors, options and risks.Trace cause and test claimsWorks downward from one undesired top event through AND and OR gates to produce a gate tree with minimal cut sets.
    TC1-04Process tracing (diagnostic tests: straw in the wind, hoop, smoking gun, doubly decisive)Trace cause and test claimsTests what each rival hypothesis predicts against diagnostic evidence within one case and produces a test result per hypothesis in a hypotheses matrix.
    TC1-05Counterfactual analysis (potential outcomes, minimal rewrite of history) Also serves: Compare.Trace cause and test claimsSets the actual outcome against an argued account of what would have happened had one factor differed and produces a counterfactual scenario.
    TC1-06Difference-in-differences Also serves: Compare.Trace cause and test claimsCompares the change over time in a treated group with the change in a control group and produces an interaction coefficient read as the treatment effect.
    TC1-07Quasi-experimental designs (natural experiment, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity)Trace cause and test claimsEstimates an effect from observational data in which exposure was assigned as if at random (natural experiment, instrument or cutoff) and produces an effect estimate under stated assumptions.
    TC1-08Confounder identification with directed acyclic graphs (adjustment sets, backdoor criterion)Trace cause and test claimsDraws assumed causal links as a directed acyclic graph and produces a diagram and an adjustment set of variables to include and to leave out.
    TC1-09Logic model and theory of changeTrace cause and test claimsMaps the expected causal chain from inputs to outcomes with the assumption behind each step and produces a logic model or theory of change.
    TC1-10Contribution analysisTrace cause and test claimsBuilds a reasoned line of evidence that a program contributed to an outcome and produces an evidenced contribution story.
    TC1-11Realist evaluation (context, mechanism, outcome configurations)Trace cause and test claimsTests what works for whom and in what circumstances through context, mechanism and outcome configurations and produces a refined program theory.
    TC1-12Process evaluation and impact evaluationTrace cause and test claimsExamines how an intervention was delivered and estimates what difference it made, and produces findings on delivery quality or an attributed effect estimate.
    TC1-13Analysis of competing hypotheses (ACH)Trace cause and test claimsLists plausible hypotheses, rates each evidence item against each, and produces a hypothesis by evidence matrix aimed at disproving hypotheses.
    TC1-14Coherence correction and averaging of probability judgementsTrace cause and test claimsAdjusts each analyst's probabilities on mutually exclusive hypotheses so they sum to one, averages across analysts, and produces a corrected aggregated judgement.
    TC1-15Bayesian updating (likelihood reasoning, likelihood ratio, Bayes factor)Trace cause and test claimsRevises the probability of a hypothesis in proportion to how much more likely the evidence is under it than under alternatives and produces a posterior probability or odds.
    TC1-16Disconfirmation search (falsification, seeking disconfirming evidence)Trace cause and test claimsLooks for evidence that would contradict a favoured claim and produces a list of refuting evidence found or not found with the search used.
    TC1-17Sensitivity analysis (robustness check, leave-one-out)Trace cause and test claimsRe-runs an analysis under different assumptions or inclusion rules and produces a table of results by assumption showing whether the conclusion reverses.
    TC1-18Internal consistency check (term from earlier work)Trace cause and test claimsTests whether the claims, figures and assumptions within one analysis are compatible with each other and produces a list of contradictions or inconsistent figures.
    TC1-19Toulmin argument analysisTrace cause and test claimsLabels the parts of an argument as claim, ground, warrant, backing, rebuttal and qualifier and produces a diagram per argument.
    TC1-20Argument mapping (argument diagramming)Trace cause and test claimsDraws claims, reasons and objections as a diagram and produces an argument map that shows isolated claims and reasons not connected to any conclusion.
    TC1-21Fallacy analysis (informal fallacies, pragma-dialectical rules)Trace cause and test claimsCompares an argument with a list of known defective patterns and produces a list of suspected defects, each read as a prompt for missing support.
    TC1-22Abduction and inference to the best explanationTrace cause and test claimsRanks candidate explanations of an observation on simplicity, generality and coherence and produces a ranked list of explanations.
    TC1-23Ladder of inferenceTrace cause and test claimsTraces how a conclusion was reached from observed data through selected data and interpretation to belief and produces a stepwise list.
    TC1-24Reflective equilibriumTrace cause and test claimsAdjusts principles and case judgements against each other until they cohere and produces a revised set of principles and judgements.
    TC1-25First principles reasoningTrace cause and test claimsReplaces inherited convention with a derivation from basic premises and produces a derivation chain with the base premises listed as assumptions.
    TC1-26Thought experimentsTrace cause and test claimsConstructs an imagined case to test a principle and produces a judgement about the case that counts as evidence on the principle only if reasoners agree.
    TC1-27Fermi estimation and order-of-magnitude checks (back-of-the-envelope estimate)Trace cause and test claimsBreaks an unknown quantity into factors that can be estimated, multiplies them, and produces a number with a plausible range used as a bound on a detailed result.
    TC1-28Dimensional and unit checksTrace cause and test claimsChecks that both sides of a relation have the same units and produces a unit balance in which a mismatch flags an error in the derivation.
    TC-01GRIM test (granularity-related inconsistency of means)Trace cause and test claimsChecks whether a reported mean of whole-number data can arise from the reported sample size and item count and produces a flag for possible or impossible means.
    TC-02SPRITE (Sample Parameter Reconstruction via Iterative TEchniques)Trace cause and test claimsReconstructs possible datasets that match reported descriptive statistics and produces plausible samples showing the range of possible item distributions.
    TC-03statcheck (recomputation of reported p-values)Trace cause and test claimsRecomputes p-values from reported test statistics and degrees of freedom and produces a list of inconsistencies and gross inconsistencies.
    TC-04Claim and citation verification against source text (fact verification; claim checking; citation support check; quotation accuracy check)Trace cause and test claimsLabels each claim as supported, refuted or not enough information against retrieved evidence, or rates whether a cited source supports its claim, and produces a label with evidence sentences.
    TC-05Subgroup and Simpson's paradox check (stratified reanalysis; confounding check; collapsibility check)Trace cause and test claimsRecomputes an association within subgroups to see whether the pooled direction reverses or vanishes and produces pooled and stratified estimates side by side.
    TC-06Ecological fallacy check (aggregation bias check)Trace cause and test claimsDecides whether a claim about people is supported by group-level data and produces a decision on whether the claim is group-level or individual-level.
    HE1-01Admiralty system (source reliability and information credibility grading)Handle evidenceAssigns each report a two-character grade for source reliability (A to F) and information credibility (1 to 6) and produces an appraised evidence base.
    HE1-02Quality of information checkHandle evidenceRates the completeness, accuracy and corroboration of each source and produces a source database with a statement of what is known and unknown.
    HE1-03CRAAP test (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose)Handle evidenceScreens one source against five criteria (currency, relevance, authority, accuracy, purpose) and produces a checklist judgement for the evidence base.
    HE1-04SIFT (stop, investigate the source, find better coverage, trace claims to the original; claim verification and fact-checking)Handle evidenceMoves from a claim to its publisher, to other coverage and to the original source, and produces a short credibility judgement with the original context located.
    HE1-05Lateral readingHandle evidenceLeaves the page to read about the publisher in other sources and produces a judgement of publisher credibility.
    HE1-06Triangulation (source, method, investigator and theory triangulation; corroboration)Handle evidenceCompares independent lines of evidence on one question and produces a convergence table of agreement, disagreement and reasons.
    HE1-07Evidence hierarchies and certainty grading (study design levels, risk-of-bias assessment, Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation or GRADE)Handle evidenceRanks study designs and rates confidence in a body of evidence from high to very low, and produces a certainty rating with reasons for downgrading.
    HE1-08Systematic reviewHandle evidenceCollects all eligible studies by a pre-specified, reproducible search, appraises and synthesises them, and produces a screening log, an evidence table and a synthesis.
    HE1-09Rapid reviewHandle evidenceRuns a systematic review with streamlined steps to meet a deadline and produces a narrative synthesis that states the shortcuts taken.
    HE1-10Scoping review (evidence mapping)Handle evidenceMaps the extent, types and gaps of evidence on a broad topic without judging the strength of findings, and produces a charted table.
    HE1-11Meta-analysisHandle evidenceCombines effect estimates from separate studies into a weighted average with a confidence interval and produces a forest plot, a pooled effect and heterogeneity statistics.
    HE1-12Narrative, thematic and qualitative evidence synthesis (including meta-ethnography)Handle evidenceCombines findings across studies in words or themes when pooling numbers is not appropriate, and produces a structured narrative or theme set linked back to studies.
    HE1-13Reflexive thematic analysis (Braun and Clarke)Handle evidenceIdentifies and interprets patterns of meaning across qualitative data in six phases and produces themes with supporting extracts.
    HE1-14Framework analysis (Framework Method)Handle evidenceSorts coded qualitative data into a case by theme matrix and produces the matrix for reading across cases and themes.
    HE1-15Content analysis (conventional, directed and summative)Handle evidenceCodes text into categories, then counts or interprets category use, and produces category frequencies or a category structure.
    HE1-16Discourse analysis (critical discourse analysis)Handle evidenceExamines how language constructs topics and positions actors and produces an interpretive account of framing checked against quoted passages.
    HE1-17Grounded theory coding (open, axial and selective coding; constant comparison)Handle evidenceBuilds categories and a theory from data through open, axial and selective coding with constant comparison and produces codes, memos and a theory.
    HE1-18Document and policy analysisHandle evidenceReads documents systematically to extract facts, positions, changes or stated reasons using their provenance and purpose, and produces an extraction table or chronology.
    HE1-19Bibliometric analysis (citation analysis, science mapping, co-citation, co-word analysis)Handle evidenceCounts and maps publications, authors and citations to show the structure and growth of a field and produces performance tables and network maps.
    HE1-20Trend and time-series decomposition Also serves: Project forward.Handle evidenceSeparates a series into trend, seasonal and irregular parts and produces plots and a fitted trend.
    HE1-21Distribution, outlier and base-rate checksHandle evidenceExamines the shape, extremes and baseline frequency of data before a figure is interpreted and produces histograms, summary statistics, flagged outliers and a base rate.
    HE-01Grey literature appraisal checklist (AACODS (Authority, Accuracy, Coverage, Objectivity, Date, Significance))Handle evidenceScores a non-peer-reviewed item on six criteria (authority, accuracy, coverage, objectivity, date, significance) and produces a completed checklist.
    HE-02Critical appraisal checklist by study design (CASP (Critical Appraisal Skills Programme) checklists; JBI (Joanna Briggs Institute) checklists)Handle evidenceAnswers design-specific questions on validity, results and relevance for one study and produces a completed checklist for the evidence base.
    HE-03Appraisal of a systematic review (AMSTAR 2 (A MeaSurement Tool to Assess systematic Reviews 2); ROBIS (Risk Of Bias In Systematic reviews))Handle evidenceRates confidence in a systematic review with AMSTAR 2 (high to critically low) or concern in four domains with ROBIS, and produces a rating or domain judgements.
    HE-04Survey disclosure completeness check (AAPOR (American Association for Public Opinion Research) disclosure elements)Handle evidenceTests whether a survey report states the sponsor, question wording, population, sampling frame, mode, dates, sample size, margin of error and weighting, and produces a list of disclosed and missing items.
