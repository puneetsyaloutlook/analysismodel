    TC1-07 Quasi-experimental designs (natural experiment, instrumental variables, regression discontinuity)

    Estimates an effect from observational data in which exposure was assigned as if at random (natural experiment, instrument or cutoff) and produces an effect estimate under stated assumptions.

    Trace cause and test claimsEvidence: UnconfirmedAnalysis technique, runs in step 5.3Strongest statement: estimate the effect of an action

    What it does

    Each uses existing data in which exposure is assigned as if at random: by an event or rule (natural experiment), by a variable that changes exposure but affects the outcome only through it (instrument), or by a cutoff (discontinuity). Artefact: an effect estimate under stated assumptions.

    When to use it

    Use when a randomised trial is impossible but an event, rule, instrument or threshold gave some units exposure for reasons unrelated to the outcome.

    When it does not fit

    Assignment is related to outcomes, the instrument is weak or has a direct effect, or units can manipulate their position relative to the cutoff.

    Result format

    The result names the design used (natural experiment, instrument or discontinuity) and describes the event, rule, variable or cutoff that assigns exposure as if at random. It gives an effect estimate and lists the assumptions under which the estimate holds.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failureAccepts an instrument or cutoff without testing its assumptions and reports a precise estimate that depends on an invalid design.
    Error sourcesinput data; statistical assumptions; data transformation
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when the instrument is weak or has a direct effect, when units manipulate the cutoff, or when the estimate applies only to a narrow group of units (risk of bias and indirectness).

    Related techniques

    • should follow TC1-08 Confounder identification with directed acyclic graphs (adjustment sets, backdoor criterion): the graph states which paths the instrument or cutoff must not open
    • substitutes for TC1-06 Difference-in-differences Also serves: Compare.: when no untreated comparison group with pre-treatment data exists (time_structure=cross-section) but an instrument or cutoff does
    • pairs with TC1-17 Sensitivity analysis (robustness check, leave-one-out): sensitivity checks vary the bandwidth, instrument and controls

    Evidence and tag basis

    Unconfirmed. The Hernán and Robins book lists instrumental variables among its topics, but the assumptions here come from general knowledge and no source on natural experiments or regression discontinuity was opened. (Hernán and Robins, Causal Inference: What If)
