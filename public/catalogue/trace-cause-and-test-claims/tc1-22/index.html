    TC1-22 Abduction and inference to the best explanation

    Ranks candidate explanations of an observation on simplicity, generality and coherence and produces a ranked list of explanations.

    Trace cause and test claimsEvidence: DocumentedPhase: analyseClaim ceiling: explain mechanism or counterfactual

    What it does

    Ranks candidate explanations of an observation and selects the best. Artefact: ranked explanations scored on simplicity, generality and coherence; a close tie signals the need for discriminating evidence.

    When to use it

    Use when you have an observation and several possible explanations and need to pick the best one or see that two are tied.

    When it does not fit

    Only poorly supported rivals were considered (the bad lot objection).

    Output specification

    The result is a ranked list of candidate explanations of the observation, with a score for each on simplicity, generality and coherence. It names the best explanation and says whether a close tie means that discriminating evidence is needed.

    Failure modes and limits

    Model suitabilityhuman judgement required
    Typical model failurePicks the most vivid or familiar explanation and does not generate serious rivals.
    Error sourcesinput data; coder or rater judgement; expert opinion
    Certainty limitsCertainty drops when the set of rivals is poor, when evidence underdetermines the choice, or when the criteria weights are not stated (risk of bias and indirectness).

    Related techniques

    • pairs with TC1-16 Disconfirmation search (falsification, seeking disconfirming evidence): a close tie needs discriminating evidence found by a targeted search
    • pairs with TC1-13 Analysis of competing hypotheses (ACH): the matrix tests the leading explanations against all evidence

    Evidence and tag basis

    Documented. Described, with objections including underdetermination and circularity. (Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy, Abduction)

    Tag basis: input profile inference; selection inference; sequencing inference; robustness inference. All tag values are inference unless a source is named.
